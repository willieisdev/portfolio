About

I'm a computational geospatial researcher at the TETFund Center of Excellence in Computational Research Intelligence, University of Uyo, where I'm also pursuing an M.Sc. in Data Science. I hold a B.Sc. in Geology (2023), and that background is where my computer vision work starts: geospatial machine learning, spatio-temporal modeling, and explainability applied to geoscience problems.

The same toolkit travels well. Whether the input is a satellite pass, a tomato leaf, a skin lesion, or a mineral thin-section, the underlying problem is the same: teaching a model to read a weak or noisy visual signal reliably. That is the thread across the projects below, and it is also the direction I am building toward: a research career at the intersection of computer vision and the geosciences.

TomatoNet

Three-task CNN classifier for tomato crops: fruit maturity detection, fruit quality grading, and leaf disease classification across ten disease classes. Each task runs its own dedicated model.

tomato-cv.streamlit.app
Live

PASSION

ResNet-50 skin-disease diagnosis model trained on the PASSION MICCAI 2024 dataset (4,901 images, 4 disease classes), reaching 80.6% test accuracy with a two-stage fine-tuning approach.

passion-skin-diagnosis.streamlit.app
Building

Mineral XAI

Explainable mineral classification from petrographic thin-sections across five classes (biotite, hornblende, plagioclase, K-feldspar, quartz), with a ResNet-50 model reaching 98.74% accuracy and Grad-CAM++/LIME explainability to surface which visual features drive each prediction.

Deploying soon
Building

Oil Spill Detection

Semantic segmentation of marine oil spills and debris from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery on the MADOS and LADOS datasets, benchmarking SegFormer-B2 and Mask2Former against the MariNeXt baseline. SegFormer-B2 reaches 0.7356 test mIoU on MADOS, about 9 points above baseline.
